Panels in the reply box, and a session row’s menu, open lined up with the button that opened them. The usage figures stand clear of Send, the reply box sits a little lower under the last line, and text is a little less bright, at macOS’s own 85% white. The new-agent box asks “What do you want to work on?”; a folder not yet chosen is its highlighted Choose folder, and why Start can’t be pressed is Start’s tooltip. A Codex session no longer jumps to the top of the list when CodeConnect restarts. Sessions message each other through CodeConnect Messages, and now act on a message from your other sessions by default, within their own permissions; “codeconnect msg-act off” makes a computer’s sessions show them to you instead, and a computer you set to off before stays off.
