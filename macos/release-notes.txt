This update changes how CodeConnect runs your agents. Update the iPhone app and CodeConnect on your other computers too: an app and a computer on different sides of the update can't connect. Agents now start from the app, and the live terminal and the codeconnect claude and codeconnect codex commands are gone. The first time the updated CodeConnect starts on this Mac, it permanently removes every run the earlier version started, with its history in CodeConnect; your paired devices, trusted folders and projects stay, and Claude Code's and Codex's own conversation files are not touched. Agents you started with the earlier version and left running keep running, but no longer appear in the app, and their permission prompts stay at the computer. End them before you update. Afterwards, an earlier version of CodeConnect can't start on this Mac.
