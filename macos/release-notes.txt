Switching accounts no longer loses a session’s waiting messages, a message sent at the moment of the switch, or its effort setting, and switching away and back quickly no longer leaves a session without its agent. A new session no longer waits for another one to end before it starts. The background service is faster and lighter: the session list answers in milliseconds even with a large history, it starts in about 50 ms, and it uses less than half the memory. The opening animation now finishes before the app appears, and a session waiting on an Edit shows the tool’s name again.
