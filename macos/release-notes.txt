The Changes panel now shows an open pull request’s checks in a Checks tab, merges it by the method you choose, and archives a finished run in its own worktree after one card says what goes. A run’s conflicts with its base lead the panel and show each file’s conflicting lines. A comment on a hunk is sent with the hunk, and a Claude worktree inside the run’s folder is no longer counted among its changes or committed with them.
